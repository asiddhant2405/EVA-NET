Graph Representation & Memory Footprint

CONCEPT B

Dijkstra Pathfinding Complexity & Live Micro-Benchmark

Compare Binary Min-Heap Priority Queue $O((V + E) \log V)$ against Naive Array Search $O(V^2)$ in real-time using performance.now().

CONCEPT C

Priority Queue & Emergency Triage Scheduling

Incidents are prioritized using a min-heap indexed by composite triage severity score $S = (\text{SafetyRating} \times 10) + \Delta t_{\text{elapsed}}$. Guarantees $O(1)$ critical incident dispatch extraction.

CONCEPT D

Network Fault-Tolerance & Vector Diff Sync

When external routing APIs (OSRM / Google) disconnect, EVA-NET seamlessly degrades to on-device localized Dijkstra with zero runtime interruptions.

EVA-NET 2.0 — Data Integrity & Provenance

Lahaina Wildfire Case Study Technical Notice

CASE STUDY GROUNDING: AUGUST 2023 LAHAINA WILDFIRE

EVA-NET 2.0 is an academic and engineering prototype designed to study algorithmic pathfinding and decentralized coordination during rapid-onset wildfire disasters. It is grounded in the geography and road network of Lahaina, Maui (August 8, 2023).

1. Verified Geographic Reference Data

Street alignments, Front Street corridor coordinates, Honoapiʻilani Highway (Route 30), and Lahaina Bypass (Route 3000) geometries are grounded in official county GIS data and OpenStreetMap records.

2. Historical Reference Data

Wildfire origins, coastal road blockages at Front St and Banyan Court, and powerline failures represent documented conditions from the August 8, 2023 disaster reports. Historical facilities (such as Lahaina Civic Center and Princess Nāhiʻenaʻena School) were actual designated shelters.

3. Simulated Operational Data

Real-time bed counts, responder units (Medic-4, Engine-6), moving evacuee agents, and SOS distress tickets are generated programmatically for demonstration and architectural evaluation.

4. Qualified Route Assessment

Routes are labeled as "Lower modeled hazard exposure based on loaded scenario data". No algorithm can guarantee 100% absolute physical safety during active catastrophic wildfire conditions with shifting gale-force winds.
